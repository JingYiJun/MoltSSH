# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in MoltSSH a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality,
appearance, disability, religion, or technical preferences.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete risks and evidence.
- Respect privacy and never post private infrastructure details, credentials,
  logs containing secrets, or personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and continue technical disagreement
  through evidence, tests, and focused proposals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Knowingly encouraging unsafe deployments or concealment of security risks.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
project communication, and public spaces where someone represents MoltSSH.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an
available private contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no
private channel is available, open a minimal issue asking for private contact
without including incident details or personal information.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that
violate this code. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent ban, depending on severity and repeated behavior. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
